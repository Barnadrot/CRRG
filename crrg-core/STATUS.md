## The runner (Phase 1a)

The runner checks are **executable and tested**, not just argued (`RUNNER.md`).
- `runner/crrg_runner.py` performs these checks, in order: the guard fields (when the manifest has a
  `guard_claim`), the lint, a separate compile, the axioms, lineage, D1 admission, the kernel commit, and R1
  derived from the move's fresh claims.
- It emits one JSON verdict with reason codes.
- The suite (`runner/tests/`, regenerated by `runner/make_tests.py`):
  - every forgery and restatement probe from the review, including Probe2 and Probe4, is rejected with the
    right code;
  - every legitimate example is accepted with the right tag;
  - there is one lint false-positive control;
  - there is one documented limit (a propositional restatement is admitted);
  - the guard-fields rule is tested on a synthetic crux family (`families/demo.json`).

## crrg-classical

`crrg-classical/` is a parallel package on Mathlib (trusted, `c5ea00351c`). crrg-core does not depend on it.
All of it is **proved**, with axioms within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`; see its README.
- **The ladder on Mathlib objects:** `PartialOrder`/`WellFoundedLT` labels, `Relation.CutExpand` steps,
  `Multiset.IsDershowitzMannaLT` frontiers, the record-low rule and `no_infinite_credits`.
- **The bridge, in both directions:** `dmlt_iff_isDM : DMLt l' l ↔ IsDershowitzMannaLT ↑l' ↑l`. The core's
  credit rule and its `no_infinite_credits` are re-derived through it.
- **State's credit machinery over multisets:** `runner_credit_sound`, `easierSucc_wf_classical`,
  `no_infinite_credits_state`.
- **Crux families on `Finset`s:** `admit_comm`, and `admitAll_perm` (any permutation of the evidence gives
  the same family), in general.
- **The classical route certificate** `CRRGClassical.CertRoute.closes`.
- **A stall and exit rule per crux** (`Stall.lean`), compatible with `no_infinite_credits`
  (`unparked_needs_conflicts`).
- **An evidence ledger** (`Ledger.lean`): recording never changes the frontier or the credit, with a
  new-ingredient gate.
- **The class-cap converse** (`Converse.lean`): `classCap_iff_listBound` for any linear code given by a
  surjective syndrome map.
- **A computed Dershowitz–Manna checker** (`DMCheck.lean`, v0.12.0): `dmCheck_sound_complete : dmCheck new record =
  true ↔ DMLt new record`, and `withComputedCert`, a proof-producing record certificate.
- **Credited labels stay at or below the root's label** (`State.lean`, v0.12.0): `record_le_root`, `credited_le_root`;
  with an unmeasured root, the first credit closes the root and is the last (`top_root_credit`, `creditOf_top_iff`).
- **Stalls with faithful lane logs** (`Stall.lean`, v0.12.0): `no_infinite_credits_stutter`, and `dichotomy`: a lane
  whose outcomes are recorded faithfully either parks or keeps learning genuinely new conflicts (content for `k ≥ 1`).
  In the gate model, each lane's entries form such a log (`gate_lane_dichotomy`, for the model only).
- **A finite region model** (`RegionProgress.lean`, v0.12.0): the root holds iff every open region holds; at most
  2|R₀| − 1 splits and closes; a separate model, not connected to the certified state.

## Honest limits

1. **Credit is conservative.** The record-low rule counts a move only against the last credited
   frontier.
   - A route that backtracks keeps its record, so it must get below it again before earning more
     credit.
   - A proposer may supply a `recordCert` (a kernel proof of the DM decrease) when the computed
     local check does not apply.
2. **Unmeasured routes earn credit only by closing leaves.** Nothing is below `top`, so refinements of
   `top` leaves are never credited, by design (review item 1).
3. **Measures are only as meaningful as the owner's families.** The core checks that a `sized` label is
   an instance of a pinned family, not that its size tracks difficulty.
4. **Runner checks are tested, not proved.**
   - The lint, lineage audit, D1 admission and R1 are executable (`runner/`), and a negative-control suite
     runs in `build.sh`.
   - They are not kernel theorems: the lint is a text check over Lean v4.30.0's meta surface, and its
     completeness is argued in `RUNNER.md`.
5. **Faithfulness and strategic value stay judgement** (CRRG Spec §10).
6. **The examples are toy stand-ins.** Instantiating `Capstone` with a real frozen target statement needs a
   downstream adapter (Mathlib side).
7. **The gate model is a design** (v0.12.0). Its steps build in that the stored state changes only by `commit`,
   `refute` or `learn`, never rolls back, and starts at `initial`. The stall dichotomy is proved for any lane of the
   model given its chronological enumeration; that a deployed log is faithfully recorded is not checked.
8. **Admission by the runner is not mechanized for self-moves.** `infinite_uncredited` covers acceptance by `commit`
   only; that the runner would also admit such moves stays an argument about tested code.
